## Datasets

- **GPT-5.4-written set** (`data/meetings`, written by gpt-5.4): 20 meetings (12 train / 8 test), ~83 turns each, 134 jobs; 19 test jobs use a held-out equipment nickname
- **GPT-6 Astra-written set** (`data/meetings-astra`, written by gpt-6-astra): 120 meetings (100 train / 20 test), ~64 turns each, 841 jobs; 54 test jobs use a held-out equipment nickname
- Trained components (GLiNER fine-tune, text classifier) use the train meetings of every writer; test meetings are never used for training.

## Caveats

- Transcripts are LLM-written from an answer key; a writer can add a deadline ("today") the key does not have, which costs every method a little on *Due*.
- Latency is per-call time summed per meeting; local models ran on a 4-core CPU without a GPU.
- Prices: `data/models.yaml` list prices; models with unknown price show `?`.
- Failed calls (timeouts) score as "found nothing" for that meeting and are listed per row. Grok 4.6 ran with a 600 s timeout (others 240 s).
- One-call (`llm_direct`) IDs are kept only when the matching words were also returned.
